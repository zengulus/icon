## Immediate correctness blockers

These are acceptance targets for the post-gate product work. Any substrate
needed to express them belongs in the active underlay phase.

### B2. Mob foes cannot exist at all — `BLOCKED` on a member model

`createFoeFromProfile` rejects the Mob role. Implement a source-backed member
representation with hit accounting, member removal, and slay suppression,
then prove a full Mob encounter. Do not simulate members as an ordinary
single-body actor.

### B3. Foe phases and chapter rules are inert data — `PARTIAL`

Profiles expose parsed phases and chapter rules, but transitions do not
execute. Add a shared transition authority with recorded triggers/results,
then wire and replay one source-exact phased Legend.

### B4. Vigilance is command-driven, not trigger-driven — `PARTIAL`

The fixture-grade `SPEND_VIGILANCE` command accepts declared results. Guard
and punish must instead derive eligibility from real damage/movement trigger
records, enforce source range, and use the existing decision and usage
machinery. Prove once-per-trigger behavior and replay.

an open implementation task.
